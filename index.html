01 / ABOUT

Physics at the scale of attoseconds.

My work focuses on how electrons respond to intense, ultrashort laser pulses — and how that motion appears in the light emitted by atoms and molecules.

I use theoretical models, numerical methods, and scientific computing to investigate the microscopic physics behind high-harmonic spectra.
